Built by Someone Who's Been There

This tool started as a personal problem.

After 21 years in the U.S. Air Force, I went through the same transition every veteran does — a stack of performance reports, a DD-214, and a civilian job market that had no idea what any of it meant.

I built Tactical 2 Talent because the tools I needed didn't exist. Not generic resume builders, not VA workshops — something that actually knew the difference between a Squadron Commander and a Flight Commander, and could explain both to a Fortune 500 recruiter in language they'd understand.

Every rule, every translation, every coached answer in this platform reflects what I learned — about what works, what doesn't, and what hiring managers actually respond to.
