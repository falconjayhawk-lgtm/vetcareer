National Advisory & Domain Strategies LLC ("Tactical 2 Talent," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of military veterans and service members who use our career transition platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use Tactical 2 Talent at tactical2talent.com or any related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By using Tactical 2 Talent, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

Section 1

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account and use Tactical 2 Talent, you may provide:

  • Name and email address (via Clerk authentication)
  • Military background: branch of service, rank, MOS/rate, years of service
  • Security clearance level and status
  • Work history, duty assignments, and accomplishments
  • Awards, decorations, and commendations
  • Educational background and certifications
  • Career preferences: target industries, desired location, work arrangement
  • Resume content and uploaded documents
  • Job tracking data and application history
  • SF-86 worksheet data (optional): Social Security number, residence history, employment history, personal references, foreign contacts, foreign travel, and relatives
  • Feedback you submit through the in-app feedback form

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect:

  • Account identifiers (user ID generated by Clerk)
  • Usage data: features accessed, searches performed, AI requests made
  • Daily request counts for rate limiting purposes
  • Log data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

Tactical 2 Talent integrates with the following services that may process your data:

  • Clerk — manages your login credentials and session tokens
  • Anthropic Claude API — processes your profile data to generate AI-powered career assistance
  • USAJobs API — searches federal job listings using your search criteria
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts our API proxy, processes requests, and stores subscription, usage, and feedback records
  • Stripe — processes subscription payments and stores billing information
  • Netlify — hosts the Tactical 2 Talent web application

1.4 Where Your Data Is Stored

Most of what you enter into Tactical 2 Talent never reaches our servers. Your profile, assignments, awards, documents, job tracker, and SF-86 worksheet are stored in your own web browser on the device you are using. We cannot read that data.

Your information leaves your browser in three cases: when you use an AI-powered feature (the relevant content is sent to Anthropic for processing), when you run a job search (your search terms are sent to the USAJobs API), and when you submit feedback (your message and user ID are stored on our servers). Account, subscription, and usage records are stored on our servers at all times.

Section 2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Tactical 2 Talent platform
  • Generate AI-powered career tools: resume assistance, LinkedIn profiles, interview prep, salary research
  • Search and surface relevant job listings from federal and civilian sources
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain account security
  • Enforce rate limits and prevent abuse of the Service
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising. We do not share your military background, clearance information, or career data with employers without your explicit action.

Section 3

Sensitive Military and Personal Information

Tactical 2 Talent collects and processes sensitive military information including security clearance levels, rank, MOS/rate, and duty assignments. We treat this information with heightened care:

  • Security clearance information is used solely to improve job search relevance and career tool accuracy
  • Clearance data is never disclosed to third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the Service
  • We do not verify clearance claims and rely solely on information you provide
  • You may omit clearance information without affecting your ability to use core features

3.1 SF-86 Worksheet and Social Security Numbers

The Service includes an optional SF-86 worksheet to help you prepare the Questionnaire for National Security Positions. This feature collects unusually sensitive information, including your Social Security number, residence and employment history, personal references, foreign contacts, foreign travel, and relatives.

  • This data stays in your browser. It is held in your browser's session storage. We do not store it on our servers and cannot read it.
  • It is cleared when you close the browser tab. Unlike the rest of your profile, SF-86 data does not persist between sessions. Use the Export function if you need to keep a copy.
  • It leaves your browser only if you choose to run an AI feature that acts on it, in which case the relevant content is sent to Anthropic for processing (see Section 10) and is not retained by us afterward.
  • The worksheet is entirely optional. Every other part of the Service works without it.
  • We are not affiliated with any government agency and do not submit anything on your behalf. This worksheet is a drafting aid only.

You acknowledge that submitting sensitive military or personal information to an AI-powered platform involves inherent risks, and you do so at your own discretion. We recommend against entering your Social Security number on a shared or public computer.

4.4 No Sale of Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing or commercial purposes.

Section 5

Data Retention

5.1 Data Stored in Your Browser

Your profile, assignments, awards, achievements, documents, and job tracker remain in your browser until you clear them, using the "Erase & Restart" function in Settings or your browser's own controls. Because we cannot read this data, we cannot delete it for you.

SF-86 worksheet data is held only for the current browser session and is cleared when you close the tab.

5.2 Data Stored on Our Servers

  • Account and subscription records (your user ID, subscription status, and Stripe customer identifier): retained while your account is active, and afterward as required for billing and tax records
  • Feedback you submit: retained for up to 12 months, stored with your user ID
  • Daily usage counters (feature and page-view counts): retained for 90 days
  • Aggregate usage totals: retained indefinitely. These are counts only and are not linked to individual users.
  • Rate limit counters: reset daily
  • Security event counts (failed sign-in attempts and similar): retained for 30 days
  • Promo code records: retained while the code is in use
  • Job search results: cached for one hour to reduce repeat processing

5.3 Data We Do Not Retain

The content you send to AI features — including resume text, profile details, and any SF-86 content you choose to process — is transmitted for the purpose of generating a response and is not stored on our servers afterward. Anthropic's handling of that data is governed by its own policies (see Section 10).

You may request deletion of your server-side data at any time. See Section 7.

Section 6

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication tokens managed by Clerk with industry-standard JWT security
  • API keys and secrets stored as environment variables, never in client-side code
  • Access to production systems restricted to authorized personnel

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and are not responsible for unauthorized access resulting from circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Section 7

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format

7.1 Deleting Your Data Yourself

Because most of your information is stored in your own browser, you control it directly:

  • Export — Settings → Data Backup & Restore → "Export All Data" gives you a portable JSON copy of your information.
  • Delete local data — Settings → Danger Zone → "Erase & Restart" clears everything stored in your browser, including your profile, documents, and job tracker.
  • SF-86 data — clears automatically when you close the browser tab.

7.2 Deleting Server-Side Data

Settings → Danger Zone → "Delete Everything" removes the records we hold on our servers — your feedback submissions, promo access grants, and cached job searches — and clears the data in your browser at the same time. This takes effect immediately.

If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it first under Settings → Manage Subscription. We block deletion while a subscription is live so that you are not charged for an account whose data has been removed.

We retain subscription and billing records after deletion where required by tax and accounting law. If you want those reviewed, or you cannot access your account, email privacy@tactical2talent.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" from your registered email address. We will confirm receipt and respond within 30 days.

We recommend reviewing Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy for details on how they handle API data.
