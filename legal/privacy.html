AfterAction LLC ("AfterAction," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of military veterans and service members who use our career transition platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use AfterAction at afteractionjobsearch.netlify.app or any related services (collectively, the "Service").

Please read this policy carefully. By using AfterAction, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Service.

Section 1

Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account and use AfterAction, you may provide:

  • Name and email address (via Clerk authentication)
  • Military background: branch of service, rank, MOS/rate, years of service
  • Security clearance level and status
  • Work history, duty assignments, and accomplishments
  • Awards, decorations, and commendations
  • Educational background and certifications
  • Career preferences: target industries, desired location, work arrangement
  • Resume content and uploaded documents
  • Job tracking data and application history

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the Service, we automatically collect:

  • Account identifiers (user ID generated by Clerk)
  • Usage data: features accessed, searches performed, AI requests made
  • Daily request counts for rate limiting purposes
  • Log data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps

1.3 Information from Third-Party Services

AfterAction integrates with the following services that may process your data:

  • Clerk — manages your login credentials and session tokens
  • Anthropic Claude API — processes your profile data to generate AI-powered career assistance
  • USAJobs API — searches federal job listings using your search criteria
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts our API proxy and processes requests
  • Supabase — stores your profile, experience, and preferences
  • Netlify — hosts the AfterAction web application
Section 2

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the AfterAction platform
  • Generate AI-powered career tools: resume assistance, LinkedIn profiles, interview prep, salary research
  • Search and surface relevant job listings from federal and civilian sources
  • Authenticate your identity and maintain account security
  • Enforce rate limits and prevent abuse of the Service
  • Communicate with you about your account
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve features and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations
We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising. We do not share your military background, clearance information, or career data with employers without your explicit action.
Section 3

Sensitive Military Information

AfterAction collects and processes sensitive military information including security clearance levels, rank, MOS/rate, and duty assignments. We treat this information with heightened care:

  • Security clearance information is used solely to improve job search relevance and career tool accuracy
  • Clearance data is never disclosed to third parties beyond what is necessary to operate the Service
  • We do not verify clearance claims and rely solely on information you provide
  • You may omit clearance information without affecting your ability to use core features

You acknowledge that submitting sensitive military information to an AI-powered platform involves inherent risks, and you do so at your own discretion.

Section 4

Data Sharing and Disclosure

4.1 Service Providers

We share your data with trusted service providers who assist in operating AfterAction, bound by confidentiality agreements: Clerk, Anthropic, Cloudflare, Supabase, and Netlify.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental authority, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights or the safety of others.

4.3 Business Transfers

If AfterAction LLC is acquired or its assets transferred, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your data becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

4.4 No Sale of Data

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing or commercial purposes.

Section 5

Data Retention

  • Account and profile data: retained until you delete your account
  • Usage and rate limit data: retained for up to 30 days for operational purposes
  • AI-generated content: not persistently stored beyond your session unless you save it
  • Uploaded documents: retained until you delete them or close your account

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@afteraction.com.

Section 6

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS
  • Authentication tokens managed by Clerk with industry-standard JWT security
  • API keys and secrets stored as environment variables, never in client-side code
  • Access to production systems restricted to authorized personnel

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and are not responsible for unauthorized access resulting from circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Section 7

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@afteraction.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Section 8

Children's Privacy

AfterAction is intended for adults who are current or former members of the United States Armed Forces. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18. If we become aware that a minor has provided personal information, we will delete it promptly.

Section 9

Third-Party Links and Integrations

AfterAction may display links to third-party job boards and external websites. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those sites. When you click a job listing and navigate to an external site (LinkedIn, USAJobs, Indeed, ClearanceJobs, etc.), that site's privacy policy governs how your data is handled.

Section 10

AI-Powered Features and Data Processing

AfterAction uses the Anthropic Claude API to power resume generation, LinkedIn profiles, interview prep, salary research, and job matching. When you use these features:

  • Your profile data and query content are transmitted to Anthropic's servers for processing
  • Anthropic processes this data pursuant to its own API usage policies
  • AI-generated outputs are provided for informational purposes and should be reviewed before use

We recommend reviewing Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy for details on how they handle API data.

Section 11

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you by updating the effective date and, where appropriate, by sending an email notification. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
